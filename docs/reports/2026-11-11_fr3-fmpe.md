# FMPE on the FR3 inverse-kinematics benchmark (`fr3_6d`, `fr3_5d`)

First FR3 numbers for the selected FMPE configuration (the `kin_fmpe_s0` setup: hand-rolled
`models/fmpe.py`, 6 gated residual blocks, ~3M parameters, `--alpha 1`, seed 0, 50 epochs).
The sampler was chosen on `split='val'` (200 poses) and only the chosen setting was scored on
`split='test'` (1000 poses). The benchmark spec is `docs/robot_ik.md`.

**Machine.** All numbers below come from one laptop: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 Laptop GPU (6 GB),
driver 621.14, CUDA 12.6, PyTorch 2.14.1+cu126, Windows 11, Python 3.12. Every time in this report
is a time on this laptop GPU (`--device cuda`). It is not comparable with CPU or cluster times of
other models; no CPU timing run was made.

## Summary

| | `fr3_6d` | `fr3_5d` |
|---|---|---|
| Chosen sampler (on val) | Euler, 64 steps (64 NFE) | Euler, 64 steps (64 NFE) |
| Test Err_post mean (FMPE / oracle) | 0.0221 / −0.0001 | 0.0032 / 0.0000 |
| Test success at 1 mm, 1° (FMPE / oracle) | 0.017 / 1.000 | 0.051 / 1.000 |
| Test success at 10 mm, 5° (FMPE / oracle) | 0.822 / 1.000 | 0.902 / 1.000 |
| Test joint-limit violation fraction | 0.084 | 0.060 |
| Calibration (marginal, joint) | calibrated, calibrated | calibrated, calibrated |
| Val loss still falling at epoch 50? | yes, log-log slope −0.158 (epochs 30–50) | yes, slope −0.088 |
| Diverged (non-finite) test samples, redrawn | 1 of 1,000,000 | 0 of 1,000,000 |

- On both tasks Err_post is far above the oracle noise floor (~5e-4): about 44× on `fr3_6d`
  and 6× on `fr3_5d`.
- Pose precision is the weak point: the median position error is 4.6 mm (6-D) and 3.3 mm (5-D), so
  few samples reach 1 mm / 1°. Most reach 10 mm / 5°.
- FMPE has no mechanism that enforces the joint limits. 8.4% (6-D) and 6.0% (5-D) of the test
  samples are outside them. Nothing was clamped or rejected.
- Neither model had converged after 50 epochs. Training was not extended (team decision, see
  open questions).

### Solver trade-off

The toy trade-off is also present on FR3. At the same NFE, midpoint has the lower Err_post (by 8e-5
to 3.7e-4, all below the 5e-4 oracle noise floor), while Euler has clearly better pose precision. At
64 NFE on `fr3_6d`, Euler has a median position error of 4.47 mm against 5.84 mm for midpoint and
2.6× its 1 mm / 1° success rate. Euler also has the lower joint-limit violation at every NFE on both
tasks.

## Diverging samples

The first `fr3_6d` test evaluation stopped with "Model produced non-finite posterior samples". The
cause is rare, and it lies in the learned velocity field, not in the solver:

- With seed 0, exactly one of the 10⁶ test samples (pose 583) was non-finite, in all seven joints.
  With seed 1, two were. All other samples stayed within |x| ≤ 5 rad.
- That sample starts from |x(0)| = 5.36, far in the tail of the 7-D standard normal. From t ≈ 0.08
  the velocity grows by a large factor at every step (|v|: 7 → 127 → 3,346 → …), reaches inf at
  t ≈ 0.44 and NaN at t ≈ 0.8.
- The same x(0) also diverges with Euler × 256 and × 2048, midpoint × 32 and × 512, and RK4 × 16 and
  × 256. With midpoint × 16 it stays finite but grows too large to take a norm of. So the ODE of the
  learned field itself diverges.
- Rate: about 0.1–0.2% of starting points with |x(0)| > 4.5, and about 1% of those with |x(0)| > 5,
  which is about 1–2 per 10⁶ samples overall.
- A possible reason, not tested here: with α = 1 the training time prior is p(t) ∝ t, so times near
  t = 0 are rarely seen, and the field there is least trained in the tails of x(0).

Decision (user): replace such samples with a fresh draw and report how many. This is
`evaluate_fmpe.py --redraw-nonfinite`. The count is stored as `n_redrawn_nonfinite` in the result
json: 1 for `fr3_6d` test, 0 for `fr3_5d` val and test. It only catches inf/NaN, not finite but huge
samples (with Euler the diverging sample goes to NaN). The `fr3_6d` validation sweep ran without the
flag; all of its samples were finite.

## `fr3_5d`: the free joint 7

On `fr3_5d`, joint 7 does not affect y and should be uniform within [−3.0508, 3.0508] rad. Over all
1000 test poses (Euler × 64, seed 0):

- Per-pose KS distance of the 1000 joint-7 samples from the uniform density: median 0.029, p95 0.047.
  For comparison, the 1000 ground-truth draws per pose give median 0.027, p95 0.043. Values outside
  the limits were moved to the nearest limit for this test.
- Pooled over all poses, the histogram is flat within about ±5% inside the range, except the two
  outermost of 20 bins, which hold 0.82× and 0.86× the flat level.
- 0.45% of the joint-7 samples are outside the limits (largest |x7| = 3.36 rad).

So FMPE learned the flat marginal of joint 7 closely; the visible defect is a soft edge at the limits,
with some mass leaking past them. The posterior figure draws the uniform line on the joint-7 panels.

## Open questions

- **Epoch budget.** Both models were still improving at epoch 50 (val log-log slopes −0.158 and
  −0.088 over epochs 30–50). Keep 50 epochs for comparability, or train longer?
- **Joint limits.** 8.4% (6-D) and 6.0% (5-D) of test samples are outside the limits. Nothing was
  clamped or rejected, so these count as violations and as failures in `success_rate`.
- **Diverging samples.** About 1 in 10⁶ samples diverges and is redrawn (counted). Other FMPE runs on
  FR3 may need the same flag. Whether α = 0 shows the same behaviour is not known; the α = 0 control
  would also answer that.
- **y noise.** Not needed: training was stable on both tasks.
